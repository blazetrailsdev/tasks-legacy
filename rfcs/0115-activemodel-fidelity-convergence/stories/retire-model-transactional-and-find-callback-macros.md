## Context

Continues the previous story. The remaining hand-written macros on
`packages/activemodel/src/model.ts`:

- `afterCommit` `:1270` (16 code lines), `afterRollback` `:1287` (16) —
  ActiveRecord's, from `activerecord/lib/active_record/transactions.rb`
  (`after_commit` / `after_rollback`), not ActiveModel's.
- `afterInitialize` `:1304` (13), `afterFind` `:1318` (13), `afterTouch` `:1332`
  (13) — generated by `activerecord/lib/active_record/callbacks.rb`'s
  `define_model_callbacks :initialize, :find, :touch`.
- `beforeValidation` `:1054` (13), `afterValidation` `:1068` (13) — these two
  **are** ActiveModel, from `validations/callbacks.rb`, whose
  `define_model_callbacks :validation, only: [:before, :after]` plus
  `_validation_callbacks`. They move to
  `packages/activemodel/src/validations/callbacks.ts`, not to activerecord.

97 code lines. Five of the seven (`afterCommit`, `afterRollback`,
`afterInitialize`, `afterFind`, `afterTouch`) are among `model.ts`'s
`parity:api:extra` rows.

`packages/activerecord/src/callbacks.ts:191` `afterFind` and `:206`
`afterInitialize` already exist as free functions, shadowed by the statics.

Two Rails details that must survive the move:

1. `after_commit` / `after_rollback` accept `on:` (`:create`, `:update`,
   `:destroy`); no other event does. `model.ts:2808` `_rejectOnOption` is the
   trails guard for this and its counterpart is the `assert_valid_transaction_
action` check in `transactions.rb`. Port the Rails check, don't carry the
   invented helper.
2. `before_validation` takes `on:` and Rails converts it to an `if:` on the
   validation context (`validations/callbacks.rb:22-31`). `model.ts:156`
   `_validationOnToIf` (25 lines) is trails' spelling of that; it belongs in
   `validations/callbacks.ts` next to the Rails code that does the same job,
   and should be converged onto that body rather than moved verbatim.

## Acceptance criteria

- `afterCommit`, `afterRollback`, `afterInitialize`, `afterFind`, `afterTouch`
  come from `defineModelCallbacks` / `activerecord/src/transactions.ts` and are
  gone from `model.ts`.
- `beforeValidation` / `afterValidation` and the `on:`→`if:` conversion live in
  `packages/activemodel/src/validations/callbacks.ts`, converged onto
  `validations/callbacks.rb:22-31`; `_validationOnToIf` and `_rejectOnOption`
  no longer exist as trails-invented module functions in `model.ts`.
- The `on:` validity check matches Rails' raise site, class and message string.
- `pnpm parity:api:extra --package activemodel` loses the five rows;
  `validations/callbacks.ts`'s `runValidations` novel row is unaffected (its
  own story).
- Parity deltas non-negative; `pnpm parity:api:calls` / `:args` clean, no
  reseed.
