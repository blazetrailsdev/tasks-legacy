## Context

Surfaced while porting the variadic `*names` shape in PR #5575.

`TableDefinition#jsonb` is declared on the **abstract** TableDefinition
(`packages/activerecord/src/connection-adapters/abstract/schema-definitions.ts`,
`jsonb(...names)` near the `json` / `char` helpers). Rails has no such method
there: the abstract `ColumnMethods` list is
`:bigint, :binary, :boolean, :date, :datetime, :decimal, :float, :integer,
:json, :string, :text, :time, :timestamp, :virtual`
(`vendor/rails/activerecord/lib/active_record/connection_adapters/abstract/schema_definitions.rb:324-328`)
— `jsonb` is PostgreSQL-only, generated by PG's own `define_column_methods`
call (`postgresql/schema_definitions.rb:185-189`).

So every adapter's TableDefinition currently answers `t.jsonb`, including
SQLite and MySQL, where Rails would raise NoMethodError.

This also has a live typing cost: because the abstract member shadows the PG
override, PR #5575 could not make the PG `jsonb` variadic without making the
abstract one variadic too — otherwise `PostgreSQLAdapter` stopped being
assignable to `AbstractAdapter`. Deleting the abstract member removes that
coupling entirely.

Same family as `abstract-tables-pg-fallback-misleading` (abstract surface that
only PG should have).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `jsonb` removed from the abstract `TableDefinition`; it exists only on
      `PostgreSQL::TableDefinition`.
- [ ] Any non-PG caller of `t.jsonb` in trails source, test-helpers, or the
      canonical schema is migrated or confirmed absent.
- [ ] PG `jsonb` keeps its variadic `*names` shape and its `AbstractAdapter`
      assignability without the abstract member propping it up.
- [ ] Green on all three lanes; parity:api extra surface drops by one.
